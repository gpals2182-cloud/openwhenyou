Hi Wyatt!

If you're reading this, I'm guessing Korea has been on your mind today.

Maybe you were thinking about the food.

Maybe you suddenly missed walking through the streets, hearing Korean everywhere, or stopping by a convenience store late at night.

Or maybe... you were thinking about the memories we made together there.

I miss all of those things too.

I miss our café dates, wandering around with no real destination, and spending hours together doing absolutely nothing.

Some of my favorite memories aren't the big moments.

They're the little ones.

Waiting for the bus together.

Walking home after dinner.

Sharing snacks from the convenience store.

Looking at the Han River at night.

Those ordinary moments became extraordinary because I was with you.

One day, we'll make new memories in Korea again.

We'll visit our favorite places, discover new cafés, eat way too much good food, and laugh about all the little things we missed while we were apart.

Until then, keep those memories safe.

They're waiting for us, just like Korea is.

I can't wait to go back there with you.

Love you always. ❤️
